Singlife (SL) vs Prudential (PRU) Integrated Shield Plans Analysis

Executive Summary

Point 1: Cancer Coverage Disparity

Singlife's Prime/Lite riders cap cancer drug services at up to 5x the MediShield Life (MSHL) limit—far below Prudential's 20x MSHL equivalents. This drop in basic cancer drug service cover (requires add-on purchase) reduces rider cost as seen in Table 1. However cancer drug service coverage is essential in Singapore's insurance system: it funds consultations, scans, lab tests, treatment prep/admin fees, supportive drugs, and transfusions tied to cancer therapies. Without it, high out-of-pocket costs persist even with subsidized CDL.

Point 2: Pricing Structure Advantage

Prudential's main Integrated Shield Plan (IP) premiums are generally lower or comparable to Singlife's across most adult age bands. In this case, for life assured age 29, and 31, 1, you see the overall medisave is 25% lower than SL, as there is no need for cross-subsidizing "free" child coverage. (Pru main plan are fair priced much lower than SL, as no recovery for free child cover needed for its adult pricing)

Actuarial Pricing Logic

Insurers calculate premiums using long-term risk pooling and mortality tables, where child policies (ages 1-20) have very low expected claims due to minimal health risks. Offering "free" coverage means waiving or heavily discounting these low premiums, but the insurer still incurs administrative costs, reserves, and minor risks. To maintain profitability and solvency, this shortfall gets recovered by inflating base rates in adult plans aligned to table below provided by MOH website.

Recovery Mechanism

The "free" child cover isn't truly free—it's cross-subsidized via higher adult contributions over the policy lifecycle, as adults pay premiums for decades while children transition to full-paying status around age 21. Prudential avoids this by charging minimal child rates outright, keeping adult premiums leaner without subsidies. This strategy attracts families to Singlife early but raises overall plan pricing, evident in comparisons where Singlife edges higher post-30s.
